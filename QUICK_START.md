# Quick Start

1. Open `index.html` in a modern browser.
2. Fill in the expense description, amount, category, and date.
3. Select **Add expense**.
4. Choose a month to view that month's total, average, category breakdown, and transactions.
5. Search or filter transactions as needed; use × to remove an entry.

The app saves expenses to browser LocalStorage automatically. Your data stays in this browser and is not synced to an account or server.

## Tips

- Amounts must be greater than zero and are rounded to the nearest cent.
- The list and summary follow the selected month.
- Change the currency selector to display totals in USD, CAD, GBP, EUR, or AUD.
- Clearing the browser's site data will also clear saved expenses.
